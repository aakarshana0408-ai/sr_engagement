# Engagement Date Panel

Asset: `engagement-date-frame-v2.webp` (1122 x 1402).

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool using the user's attached date-panel reference. Converted non-destructively from PNG to WebP at quality 90 for mobile delivery. The original generated PNG remains in the Codex generated-images directory. All invitation text is accessible live HTML, not baked into the artwork. The countdown is a separate, unchanged component below the illustrated date panel.

## Generation Prompt

Edit the supplied Tamil engagement invitation into a text-free decorative background. Preserve its warm ivory paper, fine antique-gold double outer border, floral corners, gold divider ornaments, long brass chains and hanging oil lamps with jasmine and maroon flowers, hanging bells, faint sage/peach/gold botanical scrollwork, and central raised ivory date panel with a thin ornamental floral border. Remove all typography, numbers, the clock icon, and the footer caption; seamlessly fill with matching paper. Keep the header area empty for live text and the central date panel centered. Remove the old caption area below the time and shorten only the bottom blank margin so the complete bottom ornament and border sit below the time, in an approximately 4:5 portrait canvas. Do not add garlands, thoranam, objects, flowers behind the center, or architecture.
