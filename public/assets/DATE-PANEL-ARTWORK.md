# Engagement Date Panel

Current asset: `engagement-date-countdown-v3.webp` (1050 x 1498). The earlier `engagement-date-frame-v2.webp` is retained as an unused previous version.

Created with the built-in `image_gen` tool using the user's latest attached date/countdown reference. Converted non-destructively from PNG to WebP at quality 90 for mobile delivery. The original generated PNG remains in the Codex generated-images directory. All invitation text and countdown numbers are accessible live HTML, not baked into the artwork. The existing Countdown component and event-data logic are unchanged. One image supplies the continuous paper, thoranam, hanging lamps, floral date frame, ornamental border and maroon timer panel. It renders at its natural aspect ratio without cropping, repeating or imposing a viewport height.

## Generation Prompt

Edit the latest supplied Tamil engagement invitation into a text-free decorative background. Preserve the complete 1050:1498 portrait composition: one mango-leaf thoranam with jasmine and maroon flowers, two brass kuthuvilakku lamps on long chains, fine complete antique-gold outer border, ivory textured paper, raised central gold-framed date card, rich cream flowers and sage foliage, subtle petals and gold lotus ornaments, and wide bottom maroon countdown panel with a double gold border and floral corners. Remove all lettering, numbers, labels, the clock and three internal timer separator rules for accessible live HTML overlays. Seamlessly fill those spaces with the original paper or maroon. Do not repeat, add, crop, reframe, shorten or stretch any artwork. Keep every border, lamp, flower and garland complete. Leave the date card and maroon panel blank inside.
